# مفردات: الأشياء

**اللغة:** العربية الفصحى المبسطة (MSA)

### كتاب

- Meaning: book
- Arabic: كتاب
- Transliteration: kitaab
- Part of speech: noun
- Grammar: masculine; plural كتب (`kutub`)
- Example: هذا كتاب. (`haadhaa kitaab`)

### كرسي

- Meaning: chair
- Arabic: كرسي
- Transliteration: kursiyy
- Part of speech: noun
- Grammar: masculine; plural كراسي (`karaasiyy`)
- Example: هذا كرسي. (`haadhaa kursiyy`)

### طاولة

- Meaning: table
- Arabic: طاولة
- Transliteration: taawilah
- Part of speech: noun
- Grammar: feminine; plural طاولات (`taawilaat`)
- Example: هذه طاولة. (`haadhihi taawilah`)

### بيت

- Meaning: house / home
- Arabic: بيت
- Transliteration: bayt
- Part of speech: noun
- Grammar: masculine; plural بيوت (`buyuut`)
- Example: هذا بيت. (`haadhaa bayt`)

**Usage note:** `بيت` can mean a physical house or home. The natural choice
depends on context; do not treat it as only an address or building.
