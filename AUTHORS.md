# Authors and Contributors

Language Ladder is built by people who care about practical, respectful
language learning.

## Project authors

- **umaribnanas** - project author and maintainer.

## Contributors

Contributors will be listed here as they add lessons, vocabulary, reviews,
research, structure, or other meaningful improvements. Please include the name
or handle you would like credited when opening a contribution.

Credit should reflect the work involved, including native-speaker review and
cultural guidance, not only code or file changes.

## Future maintainers

Language Ladder is intentionally open to shared stewardship. Maintainers and
long-term contributors are welcome to help guide the curriculum, review
language content, support contributors, and carry the project forward whenever
the original author is unavailable.

People who take on an ongoing role can be added here with their preferred name
or handle and the areas they help maintain.
