# Code of Conduct

Language Ladder is a learning project. Everyone should be able to participate
with curiosity, respect, and room to ask questions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of learners, native speakers, teachers, reviewers, and people from different communities.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and care, especially when discussing dialect, identity, or cultural practice.
- Welcome good-faith questions and correct mistakes without humiliation.
- Give credit for language expertise, research, and review.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the learning material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized
comments, hate speech, and deliberately misleading language guidance are not
welcome. Do not use cultural or regional differences as a reason to target
people.

## Handling concerns

If a discussion becomes unsafe or disrespectful, stop engaging and report the
concern privately to the project maintainer through the repository's available
contact or issue channel. Include enough context to understand what happened,
and avoid sharing private information publicly.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate these
standards. They should explain decisions clearly and apply the same standards
consistently.
