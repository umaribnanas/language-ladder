# Objects

These entries are concepts. Target languages may need several forms for one
concept because of gender, number, classifiers, or politeness.

### book

- Meaning: a bound set of pages used for reading
- Part of speech: noun
- Examples: This is a book.

### chair

- Meaning: a seat for one person, usually with a back
- Part of speech: noun
- Examples: This is a chair.

### house

- Meaning: a building where people live
- Part of speech: noun
- Examples: This is a house.

### door

- Meaning: a movable barrier used to open or close an entrance
- Part of speech: noun
- Examples: This is a door.
